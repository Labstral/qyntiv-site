Questions

Before
we talk.

Which businesses do you work with?

All of them: freelancers, small businesses, non-profits, public bodies and large groups, in Switzerland and abroad. What matters is a real need that off-the-shelf software covers poorly.

How much does custom software cost?

It depends on the size of the project. After the first call you receive a detailed quote: you decide knowing the exact price, with no surprises along the way.

How long does it take?

A few weeks for a focused tool, a few months for complete software. The schedule is set in the proposal, with versions to try all along the way.

Does my project stay confidential?

Yes. Your project and your information are never shared. If you wish, a non-disclosure agreement can be signed before going into detail.

What happens after delivery?

We stay by your side: fixes, updates and new features, at your business's pace.
